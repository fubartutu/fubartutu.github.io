Privacy policy – karakeep-ingest

karakeep-ingest is a personal tool used only by its author.

  • It requests read-only access to YouTube (youtube.readonly) to read the author's own "Liked videos" list.
  • For each liked video it stores only the video link and title, in the author's self-hosted Karakeep instance.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/fubartutu/fubartutu.github.io.
